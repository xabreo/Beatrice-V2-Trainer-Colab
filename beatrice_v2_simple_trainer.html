Beatrice V2 Simple Trainer

A simple wrapper around the official Beatrice V2 training workflow. Beatrice remains the underlying training engine.

Upstream stays upstream. This project provides a simpler user journey around the existing trainer. It does not claim ownership of Beatrice or replace its training implementation.
